# W5 · Tighter courier batching

Author: Joud Al-Fahad. Test unit: customer.

## Summary

Batching two orders per trip cut delivery cost per order by 6% (95% interval 4% to 8%) in a city-by-hour switchback. Courier earnings per online hour fell by 4.2% (95% interval -5.9% to -2.5%); the guardrail allowed -2%. We recommend launch because the cost saving is large.
